The HILL Lab welcomes students who are excited about natural language processing and
human-centered AI.

## Prospective PhD Students
If you are applying to the PhD program in Computer Science at Texas State University and
are interested in NLP, LLM safety, or computational social science, please apply through
the [Graduate College](https://www.gradcollege.txst.edu/) and mention your interest in
the HILL Lab in your application. You are welcome to email Dr. Perez-Rosas with your CV
and a short note about your research interests.

## Current Texas State Students
Current MS and undergraduate students interested in research are encouraged to reach out.
Please include your background, relevant coursework, and what you would like to work on.

## What we look for
- Curiosity about language and how people use it
- Strong programming skills (Python) and a willingness to learn
- Interest in NLP, machine learning, or computational social science

To get in touch, see the [Contact page](/contact/).
