The **Human Interaction in Language and Learning (HILL) Lab** is a research group at
[Texas State University](https://www.txst.edu/), directed by
[Dr. Veronica Perez-Rosas](/people/). We work at the intersection of
**natural language processing**, **computational social science**, and
**human-centered AI**, building systems that understand how people communicate and
that support learning, well-being, and trust in language technologies.

Our research spans multimodal and conversational NLP, deception and misinformation
detection, large language model safety and evaluation, and NLP for social good.
Faculty and students collaborate across computer science, linguistics, psychology,
and education to study language as it is actually used by people.

Research areas
======
- **Conversational & multimodal NLP** — modeling dialogue, affect, and behavior across text, speech, and vision.
- **Deception, misinformation & trust** — detecting deceptive, manipulative, and machine-generated language.
- **LLM safety & evaluation** — probing, benchmarking, and improving the robustness of large language models.
- **NLP for social good** — applying language technologies to health, education, and well-being.

See the [Research page](/research/) for details, and the [Publications page](/publications/)
for our latest papers.
