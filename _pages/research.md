The HILL Lab studies how people use language and how language technologies can better
serve human needs. Our work combines natural language processing, machine learning,
and computational social science. Current directions include:

## Conversational & Multimodal NLP
Modeling dialogue, affect, and human behavior across text, speech, and vision — including
how people express intent, emotion, and rapport in interaction.

## Deception, Misinformation & Trust
Detecting deceptive, manipulative, and machine-generated language, and understanding the
linguistic signals that distinguish authentic from fabricated content.

## LLM Safety & Evaluation
Probing, benchmarking, and improving the robustness and reliability of large language
models, with an emphasis on trustworthy and safe deployment.

## NLP for Social Good
Applying language technologies to problems in health, education, and well-being, and
studying how these systems affect the people who use them.
